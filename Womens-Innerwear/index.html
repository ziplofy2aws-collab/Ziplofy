Reviews

Excellent support & comfort for running

I've been a runner and vainly searching for good running bras for 20+ yrs. Delighted with this bra, my search has finally ended. I am a small band/big cup combo, which thankfully this bra offers—wd request a 30 band size too.

The straps are detachable and can be worn cross back for greater support, which is what I do. Only thing, once one strap slipped out of the loop—needs to fit more tightly. Otherwise, fit, support and comfort all just superb. Have worn it on three runs so far.

Anjali M.

Everyday comfort that actually lasts

Soft fabric, no digging straps, and the shape stays put through long workdays. I've washed it several times and it still looks new.

Finally found something between flimsy lounge bras and heavy sports styles. Highly recommend for daily wear.

Priya S.
