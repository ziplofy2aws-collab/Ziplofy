Getting Started

To create your first store, sign up at auth.codiic.com, then click "Create Store" from your dashboard. Enter your store name and select a theme. Codiic will guide you through adding products, setting up payments, and launching your storeall within minutes.

Billing & Plans

Plans are billed monthly or annually. You're charged at the start of each billing cycle. Payment methods can be updated in Settings Billing. Invoices are emailed and available in the dashboard.

Was this helpful?
Thank you!

Go to Settings Billing. Choose your new plan and confirm. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the next billing cycle. Prorated credits may apply when upgrading.

Integrations

Codiic integrates with payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal), email (Mailchimp, Klaviyo), analytics, shipping providers, and more. Visit Integrations in your dashboard to connect apps with one click.

Account & Security

On the login page, click "Forgot password." Enter your email and we'll send a reset link. The link expires in 1 hour. If you don't see it, check spam or request a new one.
