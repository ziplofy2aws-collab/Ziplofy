FAQS – Driphaus

Our beans are small-batch roasted to order, sourced as single-origin microlots, and traceable to partner farms—so you get transparent flavor notes, peak freshness, and specialty-grade quality in every bag.

Yes. We roast profiles for espresso, pour-over, French press, AeroPress, and cold brew. Each listing notes the recommended brew method and roast level so you can pick the right bean for your setup.

Beans are roasted after you order and shipped within 24–48 hours from our roastery. You’ll typically receive them at peak freshness, ideal for grinding and brewing within two to four weeks.

Choose whole bean or pre-ground options for espresso, filter, French press, or cold brew. We recommend whole bean for maximum freshness and grind just before brewing whenever possible.
