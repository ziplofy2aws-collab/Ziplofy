Product Description

This lightweight, oil-free moisturizer is powered with 3% NMF Complex and Panthenol to deliver up to 72-hour hydration without clogging pores. It soothes, calms, and restores the skin barrier, making it ideal for oily, acne-prone, and sensitive skin. The non-comedogenic, non-sticky formula gets absorbed quickly and leaves skin feeling fresh and balanced all day long. It contains no added fragrance, parabens, or alcohol, ensuring a gentle experience.

Why Is This Different?

The NMF complex is a mixture of amino acids and other derivatives that sustain hydration in the stratum corneum (uppermost layer of skin). Acting as a non-comedogenic moisturiser (humectant mixture), it retains moisture while strengthening the skin barrier. The amount of NMF in the Stratum corneum determines how much water it can hold at a particular humidity level. Panthenol, a non-oily skin hydrator, prevents transepidermal water loss and also soothes skin and works well for oily/acne-prone skin.

Our oil free face moisturiser for oily skin is uniquely designed to provide hydration without making the skin greasy. Here's why it stands out:

How Does It Work On The Skin?

  • Hydration: The 3% NMF Complex, rich in amino acids and minerals, mimics the skin's natural moisturising factors to keep the skin deeply hydrated and nourished.
  • Skin Barrier Strengthening: Panthenol (Vitamin B5) works to repair and protect the skin barrier, preventing moisture loss.
