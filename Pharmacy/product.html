Description

Protinex is a high protein nutritional supplement for adults that helps build strength, supports immunity and helps you stay active. It contains hydrolysed protein that is easily digested and absorbed. Original flavour — mix with milk or water for a nourishing drink as part of a balanced diet.

Key Benefits

  • Helps build strength and supports an active lifestyle
  • Supports immunity with essential nutrients
  • Contains hydrolysed protein for easy digestion

Direction for Use/Dosage

Take 2 heaped tablespoons (approx. 25 g) of Protinex in a glass of lukewarm milk or water, stir well and consume. Recommended once or twice daily or as directed by a physician.

Safety Information/Precaution

Not for medicinal use. Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Keep out of reach of children.

Other Information

Protinex Original is a vegetarian product suitable for adults seeking additional protein in their diet. Consult your doctor before use if you are pregnant, nursing or have a medical condition.
