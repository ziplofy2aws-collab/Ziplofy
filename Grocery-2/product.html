        Introduction

        For centuries, cow ghee has held a sacred place in Indian culture and traditions. Made with care using ancient methods, A2 cow ghee is prized for its purity, health benefits and rich aroma. Crafted from the milk of indigenous desi cows, it is naturally golden, deeply nourishing and free from artificial additives.

        Beyond its culinary uses, ghee is celebrated in Ayurveda for supporting digestion, immunity and overall wellbeing. A spoonful added to your daily meals enhances both flavour and nutrition — a wholesome, time-honoured superfood that has been passed down through generations.

        At FreshHaat, every batch is prepared in small quantities to preserve its authentic taste and goodness. Whether used in cooking, baking or as a daily supplement, our ghee brings the comfort of tradition and the assurance of quality to your kitchen.
