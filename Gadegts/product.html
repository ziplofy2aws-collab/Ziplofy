Tech Specifications

The AETHER 737 Power Bank (PowerCore 24K) delivers laptop-grade charging in a compact, travel-ready design. Intelligent power allocation detects your device type and delivers optimal wattage across every port.

Built with GaN technology and a smart digital display, you can monitor output, input power, and estimated recharge time at a glance. Power Delivery 3.1 support keeps MacBook, iPad, iPhone, and Android devices charged from a single pack.
