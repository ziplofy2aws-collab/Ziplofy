  1. 1

    Measure & Grind

    Use 18g of whole beans per cup and grind fresh just before brewing for the best aroma.

  2. 2

    Heat the Water

    Bring filtered water to 92–96°C — just off the boil for a balanced extraction.

  3. 3

    Brew

    Steep for 3–4 minutes (French press) or pull a 25–30 second espresso shot.

  4. 4

    Enjoy

    Pour, savor the rich crema and bold finish. Best enjoyed black or with a splash of milk.
